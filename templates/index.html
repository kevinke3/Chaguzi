How Chaguzi Works

From registration to announcement — a complete, transparent election workflow.

1

Register Your Organization

Administrators register their school, church, company, or SACCO with all necessary details.

2

Voters Join

Members register under their organization using their membership or employee ID.

3

Create Elections

Set up elections, positions, candidates, schedules, and result visibility options.

4

Vote Securely

Each voter casts one vote per position. Duplicate votes are blocked at every layer.

5

Watch Live Results

Optional live results or results after close — your choice, clearly visualized.

6

Announce Winners

Review final results, announce winners, and preserve a full audit history.

Secure by Design

Hashed passwords, CSRF protection, parameterized queries, unique vote constraints, and role-based access.

Multi-Organization

Each organization sees only its own voters, elections, candidates, and results.

Live & Final Results

Choose to keep results hidden, show them live, or reveal after the election closes.

Responsive

Clean, modern UI that works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
